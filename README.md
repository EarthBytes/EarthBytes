### About

I’m an AI/ML & Full-Stack Engineer who enjoys building practical AI systems and exploring what’s possible with machine learning.

I’m particularly interested in LLMs, RAG, AI agents, deep learning and generative AI. I enjoy experimenting with models, understanding how they work, and building the systems around them to turn ideas into useful applications.

Alongside AI/ML, I enjoy working across the full stack - from frontend and backend development to databases, cloud infrastructure, deployment and CI/CD. I like being able to take an idea from an initial prototype through to a complete, deployed application.

I’m naturally curious and learn best by building, experimenting with new technologies, and working on projects that push me to understand something new.
